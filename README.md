# Invoice Generator

**Free online invoice generator — no login, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking.**
Create, customize, and export professional invoices to PDF right in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

## Features

- **Edit directly on the invoice** — click any field to type
- **Logos** — your business logo + client logo (auto-fit, removable)
- **QR code** with a built-in cropper (great for payment QRs from screenshots)
- **Payment banner** — Wise, PayPal, Stripe, Venmo, Cash App, Bank Transfer, or custom color, with a clickable **PAY NOW** link
- **Totals** — Tax & Discount (toggle % or amount), Shipping, Amount Paid, Balance Due
- **Notes & Terms** sections
- **Themes** — 5 presets + custom color picker
- **Page sizes** — A4, Letter, Legal, A5
- **Export to PDF** (uses the browser's print-to-PDF)
- **Autosave** — your draft is cached in your browser, so an accidental close never loses your work
- **Responsive** — works on desktop and mobile

## Usage

Open `index.html` (or the hosted URL). Everything runs client-side — no server, no database, no account.

## Hosting

It's a single static file. Deploy to any static host:

- **Cloudflare Pages**, **Netlify**, **Vercel**, or **GitHub Pages**

No build step required — just serve `index.html`.

## Privacy

All data (including the autosaved draft) lives only in your browser's local storage. Nothing is ever uploaded.
